THE EXPERIENCE

Designed to be clear, considered and trustworthy

These design principles can be evaluated independently of live commerce infrastructure.

01

Market and language independence

Display language, reading direction and selected market are separate concepts.

02

Mobile-first usability

Layouts prioritize legibility, spacing and straightforward navigation on smaller screens.

03

Transparent information

Until live data is verified, no price, stock level or delivery promise is invented.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Straightforward answers

Can I buy any of these products?

No. All cards are illustrative samples for evaluating the layout and browsing experience.

Does switching countries show real prices?

No. It only changes the example collections and displays the market's currency code. No conversion or live pricing occurs.

Is signup or payment enabled?

No. This preview is not connected to customer accounts, inventory, payment providers or order submission.

When will the store be live?

After the backend is running, integrations and required agreements are verified, and end-to-end tests pass. No launch date is confirmed.
